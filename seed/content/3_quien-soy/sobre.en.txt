Bio: Street and documentary photography in colour. Madrid and Cantabria.

----

Texto: I work from the edge of a scene rather than its centre. More often than not I end up photographing what people leave behind rather than what they do. Self-taught. I live in the mountains outside Madrid, and most of these pictures were made a few kilometres from home, or in Cantabria. I'm more interested in atmospheres than in stories.

----

Descripcion: Nico Tobias photographs the street in colour in Madrid and Cantabria. Contact.
